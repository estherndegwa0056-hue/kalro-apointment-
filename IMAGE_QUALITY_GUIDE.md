## Best format

For the highest practical website quality, provide **WebP or high-quality JPG** originals. Keep important subjects away from the extreme edges because product cards crop images. Avoid adding text or watermarks to the photos.

## Simple replacement process

1. Open `assets/site-images/`.
2. Replace a file with your own photo using the **same filename**.
3. Keep the extension (`.jpg`) unless you also update the HTML/JavaScript reference.
4. Refresh the website.
5. Click the image to verify the full-resolution version opens.
